Privacy policy

Sudoku Family · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Sudoku Family is published by WORLDMEDIA. This policy describes the current version of the app.

Data and saved progress

The game does not require an account. It stores completed levels, entered numbers, notes, play time, challenge times, your chosen player name and hint use on your device so you can resume a game and compare results. This information remains in the app’s local storage. The publisher does not receive it.

Connectivity and external services

Puzzles and game resources are included in the app. The game works offline and has no ads, analytics or account service.

If you choose to share a challenge or a result, the code, chosen name and time shown are sent through the sharing app you select. You initiate this sharing.

Subscription

After five completed free games, the app offers a monthly subscription. Purchases, renewals and restorations are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. Those stores apply their own privacy rules; WORLDMEDIA does not receive your payment card details through the app.

Deletion

To remove locally stored progress, clear the app’s data in your device settings or uninstall the app. Any operating-system backups are controlled by your device’s backup settings.

Children

Children and adults can use the game. The publisher does not collect personal data through the app.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email contact@worldmediaa.fr.

Changes

We will update this policy if the game or its data practices change.
